# Milestone Plans Index

This directory contains elaborated plans for the major sub-version upgrades of `tlm`. The roadmap is structured progressively to ensure a stable foundation, followed by extensibility, and concluding with high-level autonomy and distribution.

## Progressive Roadmap

### Wave 1: Foundation & Stability
1. [v0.3.0: Foundation (Safety & Streams)](./v0.3.0_foundation.md)
   - Tiered permissions, **Temporal Snapshotting**, and **Password/Recovery Foundation**.
2. [v0.4.0: Capabilities (Abilities & Containment)](./v0.4.0_capabilities.md)
   - Source-built Integrated Abilities and non-destructive Extensions.

### Wave 2: Intelligence & Persistence
3. [v0.5.0: Optimization (Model Router & Provider Hub)](./v0.5.0_optimization.md)
   - **Order Dispatcher**, Benchmarking, and Cost Optimization.
4. [v0.6.0: Persistence (Advanced Memory & Worker State)](./v0.6.0_memory.md)
   - **Digital Twin** Memory and **Modular Sensory System**.

### Wave 3: Interface & Orchestration
5. [v0.7.0: Interface (WebUI & Observation Deck)](./v0.7.0_interface.md)
   - **Unified Configuration Hub** (TUI/WebUI/GUI) and **Sentient Self-Setup**.
   - Observation Deck and Heartbeat Diagnostics.
6. [v0.8.0: Orchestration (Quad & Blueprints)](./v0.8.0_orchestration.md)
   - Agent Quad, The Governor, and Cryptographic Blueprints.

### Wave 4: Sentience & Distribution
7. [v0.9.0: The Sentient Heart (Personality & Autonomy)](./v0.9.0_heart.md)
   - Persistent **Personality Engine** and Human-Like interaction.
   - **Ghost Mode** (Speculative Pre-Computation).
   - **Synthetic Self-Correction** (Learning from post-mortems).
   - Social Bridges (Discord/Telegram).
8. [v0.10.0: Distribution (Enterprise & Packaging)](./v0.10.0_distribution.md)
   - Global packaging (Homebrew, AUR, Scoop, Winget).
   - **Verified Blueprint Registry** with signed certificates.
   - System keyring integration and production safety profiles.
